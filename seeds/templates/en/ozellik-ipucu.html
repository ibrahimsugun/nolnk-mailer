Your NoLnk account is ready to go, but a few corners of the dashboard are easy to miss. Here are three tips, each a couple of minutes' work:
1  Turn a link into a QR code
Need it on a poster, a menu or a business card? Generate the QR code straight from the dashboard. Depending on your plan you can keep it dynamic (change the destination later) or give it your own styling.
2  Send links from your own domain
Add a branded domain and your short links carry your name instead of ours. A familiar domain earns trust before anyone clicks.
3  Ask your analytics one question
Open a link's analytics, narrow the time range, then look at the country, device and browser breakdown. "Which channel actually worked?" usually answers itself right there.
